Common questions

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

Open Glissade, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What does Remove Ads include?

It takes away every ad and gives you unlimited hints, undos and skips. It’s a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

I’m stuck on a level. What can I do?

Tap Hint for a nudge. Levels give you a few free hints and undos, and after that you can watch a short video for more, or get Remove Ads. If a level keeps giving you trouble, a skip option will appear.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Glissade has no accounts and doesn’t sync between devices. If you move to a new phone, your progress comes along when you restore it from a device backup (iCloud or your computer).

How do I start over?

Settings › Reset Progress. This clears your levels and stars. It doesn’t affect anything you’ve bought.

Can I leave a tip?

Thank you! Open Settings and tap Support Me. Tips are optional and don’t unlock anything. They simply help me keep making levels.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
