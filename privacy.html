Glissade

Privacy Policy

Glissade · Last updated November 9, 2026

Glissade is a puzzle game made by Joseph Pope, an independent developer (“I” or “me”). This page explains what information the game uses and who handles it. If anything is unclear, write to me at blindvicegerent@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Glissade has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. I don’t run any servers that receive them.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to do that. The details and your choices are below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. I never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

The game saves which levels you’ve cleared, your star ratings, your daily puzzle progress and streak, your settings (sound, haptics, higher contrast and the daily reminder), and whether you’ve bought Remove Ads or seen the end-of-game thank-you. This lets the game remember where you left off. I don’t receive it. Deleting the game removes it from your device, though it may also sit in your device’s own backups (such as iCloud Backup), which Apple manages.

Daily reminder

The daily reminder is optional and off unless you turn it on. If you do, the game asks iOS for permission to send notifications and then schedules a short reminder on your device for 7 PM on days when the daily puzzle isn’t done yet. Your device creates and shows the reminder. Nothing is sent to me or to any notification server. You can turn it off at any time in the game under Settings › Daily Puzzle, or in iOS Settings under Notifications › Glissade.

Ads

Glissade shows video ads, which you can remove with the Remove Ads purchase. The ads come from Google AdMob, through Google’s Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow it), your IP address, details about your device and its operating system, and how you interact with ads. If you allow it, this information may also be used to make ads more relevant to you. I don’t receive this information in a form that identifies you.

You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Once you buy Remove Ads, the game stops showing ads and no longer starts Google’s ads service when it opens.

Your choices

  • When you first open the game, iOS may ask whether Glissade can track you across other companies’ apps and websites. You can say no, and the game works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the game may show a consent message about ads. Where that applies, you can change your choices later in the game under Settings › Ad Privacy Settings.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop ads altogether.

Purchases

Remove Ads and the optional thank-you purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and does not share your payment details with me. The game only asks Apple whether you’ve bought Remove Ads, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy.

If you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you and to improve the game, and I don’t share it. If you’d like me to delete our conversation, just ask.

Children

Glissade is not aimed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the game handles information, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
